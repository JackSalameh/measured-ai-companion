## What each file contains

**tickets.csv.** 600 fictional support tickets dated 1 January to 30 June 2026, plus 18 resubmissions (identical text, a few days later): ticket_id, created, channel (email, chat, phone), tier (free, pro, team), text, queue (billing, login, shipping, other). Planted: missing channel values; resubmitted duplicates.

**ledger.csv.** Double-entry journal of a fictional company, Alder and Finch Trading, for 2026: 360 transactions plus one entered twice, two lines each (debit and credit), amounts in EUR. Planted: one transaction entered twice (new ID, same content); three missing descriptions; one impossible date (2026-02-30). Debits equal credits in total.

**signal_features.csv.** 120 one-second records of synthetic motor vibration and current, sampled at 12,000 samples per second (one record per minute; every fourth record faulty). Columns: vibration RMS, peak, crest factor and kurtosis; current RMS; temperature; unit; label. Raw signals are regenerated by `gen_signals.record(k, faulty)`. Planted: three records logged in g instead of m/s²; two missing temperatures. Teaching signals only; they model no real machine.

## Intended use and limits

For teaching data handling, evaluation and leakage. The data are synthetic: patterns in them were put there by the generators and say nothing about real customers, companies or machines.
